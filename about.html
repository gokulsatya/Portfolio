About

Hi, I’m Gokul Sathiyamurthy — a cybersecurity professional with 3+ years across vulnerability management, threat monitoring, and incident response. My track record includes 1,000+ MITRE ATT&CK-mapped threat hunts, 100+ detection rules in KQL, YAML, and JSON, SIEM monitoring with Splunk and Microsoft Defender EDR/MDR, and hands-on OT/ICS lab experience with Wazuh, GrassMarlin, and OpenVAS. These days I’m also building Azure AI Foundry agent workflows in Python at Synergech.

I’m someone who thrives on challenges. Whenever someone tells me something is impossible, my first thought is always “how can I make this possible?” That mindset drives me in both my personal and professional life.

Outside of work, I’m passionate about sports and fitness. Cricket is one of my favorite activities, and I make time to hit the gym for at least an hour every day — staying active keeps me balanced, physically and mentally.

On the football field I play as a defender. I didn’t score many goals in that position, but I took pride in preventing the opposition from scoring. Looking back, it’s funny how that defensive mindset may have shaped my career — perhaps that’s what drew me to become a Defender endpoint expert in cybersecurity.

When I’m not working or playing sports, I enjoy pushing my boundaries and taking on new challenges. I believe growth comes from stepping outside your comfort zone — in fitness, in sports, and in professional development.
